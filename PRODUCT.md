# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wedding photography studio owners and sales staff. They use this at a desk between enquiries, not on a marketing site. The job is to assemble a priced proposal for a couple and export a PDF they can send.

## Product Purpose

Framewise is an internal sales tool: catalog of services and packages, customer records, draft proposals with server-side pricing, and PDF export. Success is a quote that is accurate, on-brand, and out the door without spreadsheet math.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, filmic, precise. Studio language, not wedding-fair language. Confidence comes from craft and numbers, not ornament.

## Anti-references

Wedding-template cream and script serifs. SaaS purple dashboards. Glass login cards over mesh gradients. Generic spinning 3D primitives (cubes, torus knots, particle fields) with no relation to film or tape.

## Design Principles

- The tool disappears into the task; surprise is saved for entry moments, not every screen.
- Price and catalog truth live on the server; the UI never pretends to own the total.
- Photography craft can show up as material and light, not as clip-art hearts or gold foil.
- One accent, used for action. State changes are the motion that matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on text, inputs, and primary actions. Visible focus rings. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops decorative 3D animation and keeps a still frame. Form errors are inline text, not color alone.
